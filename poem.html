47

Months of us

I could count our love in months,
in mornings, photographs, and nights—
but none of those could ever hold
the way you changed my life.

You made the ordinary beautiful:
the coffee, drives, and tired days,
the walks with Maya, quiet rooms,
and all our strange and laughing ways.

You have known the parts of me
that words don’t always show,
and somehow, through each changing season,
you still decided not to go.

So after three years and eleven months,
here is the simplest truth I know:
I would choose you at the beginning,
and I would choose you as we grow.

I choose you in the easy moments,
and when the world feels hard to face.
I choose you for the life ahead—
my favorite person, my safest place.

This isn’t only a celebration of how long we’ve loved each other.
It’s my promise that I’m still excited about everything that comes next.
Forever yours, Ben ♥
Now come get your flowers and coffee.
I’m taking you to breakfast.
Made only for Bella • 3 years and 11 months
